# code-tutor Glossary

- **Region:** A major subject area on the learning map.
- **Landmark:** A focused learning destination within a region.
- **Canonical content:** The authoritative version of learning material.
- **Manifest:** An index that identifies the available content.
- **Access seam:** The boundary where access rules are applied.
- **Entitlement:** A granted right to access a product capability or content.
- **Trial:** A time-limited period of access.
- **Snapshot:** A saved, shareable representation of a point-in-time state.
